Answers to Your Inquiries

  • What is Check in Check Out Time?

    Check in is at 10 AM, check out is at 12 Noon.

  • Do you allow smoking on property? In the rooms?

    Our hotel is entirely non-smoking.

  • Do you offer room service? What are the hours?

    Yes, we offer In Room Dining 24 hours a day, with a limited overnight menu.

  • Do you have a pool?

    Yes, our two pools are located on our 16th floor rooftop.

  • How do I get to the hotel?

    Please see our Maps + Directions page on Google.

  • I booked on a website other than the hotel website and I need to make a change to my reservation. How do I do this?

    In this situation, you would have to contact the customer service department at the website you used to make your reservation.

  • Will your rooms allow rollaway beds/cribs/playards? Is there a charge?

    Some of our rooms can accommodate cribs or rollaway beds. There is a per night rental fee for all rollaway beds.

  • Is my ID required at check in?

    We do require presentation of a valid government-issued identification at the time of check-in.

  • Can I check in with my debit card?

    We absolutely take debit cards at check-in.

  • What forms of payment are accepted to pay for my room?

    All major credit cards are accepted.
